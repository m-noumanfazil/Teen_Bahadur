# NEDUET Shuttle Points and Routes

## Points

### Main Gate
The main entry and exit point of the NEDUET campus.

### Library
The central library for all students, located near the main campus buildings.

### Computer Science Department
Houses the computer science faculty and labs.

### Electrical Engineering Department
Contains the electrical engineering classrooms and workshops.

### Mechanical Engineering Department
Workshops and classrooms for mechanical engineering.

### Civil Engineering Department
Dedicated facilities for civil engineering studies.

### Student Hostel
Accommodation for students within the campus.

### Faculty Hostel
Accommodation for faculty members within the campus.

## Routes

### Route 1
Main Gate -> Library -> Computer Science Department -> Electrical Engineering Department -> Main Gate

### Route 2
Main Gate -> Mechanical Engineering Department -> Civil Engineering Department -> Student Hostel -> Faculty Hostel -> Main Gate
